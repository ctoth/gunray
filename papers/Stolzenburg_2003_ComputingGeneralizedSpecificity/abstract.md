# Abstract

## Original Text (Short Excerpt)

The paper studies an autonomous specificity-based comparison criterion for contradictory conclusions in rule-based defeasible reasoning.

---

## Our Interpretation

The paper argues that specificity should compare complete arguments in their strict-background context, not just fixed priority pairs over defeasible rules. It gives activation-set and path-set machinery that makes generalized specificity implementable and testable in DeLP-style systems such as Gunray.
